The Sheep A living storybook

Click the clasp to open the book. Use the lower-left page corner for a new flock and the lower-right corner for the next season.

Hold a sheep until its wool expands, then release to turn it into a cloud. The sheep leaves the meadow.

Drag a cloud and release it to let it drift again. Some clouds produce rain, gradually creating a new sheep.

Click empty grass to add a sheep. Drag up or down to adjust daylight. Move the pointer around during the day to change shadow directions.

Click the cassette to play or pause, and the small arrow beside it to restart the recording. Sheep dance at night; during the day, wolf shadows emerge as wolves and chase the sheep.

Keyboard: Enter to open · ← new flock · → next season · N day/night · M music · T restart recording · H help. Day and night also change gradually over time.
